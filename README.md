# Financial Data Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Financial Data Analysis Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze and visualize financial performance across multiple companies/stocks over different years.

The dashboard provides insights into key financial metrics such as **revenue, net income, total assets, total liabilities, and cost of revenue**. It allows users to interact with the data using year filters and explore financial performance across different stocks.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze the financial performance of different companies.
* Compare **total revenue and net income** across stocks.
* Identify companies with higher **net income**.
* Analyze **cost of revenue** by stock.
* Track annual revenue trends.
* Understand the relationship between revenue and profitability.
* Present financial information through an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Calculated measures and financial KPIs
* **Data Modeling** – Relationships between financial tables
* **Data Visualization** – Charts, KPI cards, slicers, and interactive reports

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### 1. Financial KPI Overview

The dashboard displays important financial indicators:

* **Total Revenue Annually**
* **Total Net Income Annually**
* **Total Assets**
* **Total Liabilities**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall financial position.

### 2. Net Income by Stock

A horizontal bar chart is used to compare **net income across different stocks**. This helps identify differences in profitability among companies.

### 3. Cost of Revenue by Stock

The dashboard visualizes the **cost of revenue for individual stocks**, allowing users to compare the expenses associated with generating revenue.

### 4. Revenue vs Net Income

A comparison chart shows **total revenue and net income by stock**, helping analyze the relationship between a company's revenue generation and profitability.

### 5. Annual Revenue Analysis

The dashboard includes an annual revenue visualization to understand changes in revenue across different stocks and financial periods.

### 6. Year Filter

An interactive **Year slicer** allows users to select a specific year and dynamically update the dashboard visuals.

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify:

* Companies generating higher revenue.
* Companies with comparatively higher net income.
* Differences between revenue and profitability.
* Stocks with higher costs of revenue.
* Changes in financial performance across different years.
* Overall financial position based on assets and liabilities.
